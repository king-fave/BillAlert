About BillsAlert

our story

Founded in 2023, BillsAlert emerged from a simple vision: to make bill payments and digital services accessible to everyone in Nigeria and beyond.
We understand the challenges that come with managing multiple bills and subscriptions. That's why we've built a platform that simplifies these processes, making them faster, more secure, and more convenient.
Today, we serve thousands of satisfied customers, processing millions of transactions securely and efficiently.
